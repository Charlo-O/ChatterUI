# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile users who chat with local or remote large-language models, manage character cards, switch among conversations, and tune inference or API settings. The primary context is repeated, focused use on a phone where the conversation should remain more prominent than the controls.

## Product Purpose

ChatterUI is a native mobile frontend for running on-device models or connecting to LLM APIs. It gives users a dependable place to manage characters, conversations, models, connections, prompts, samplers, TTS, and application settings without hiding expert controls.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, clear, and calm. The interface should feel native and deliberate, with strong black-and-white hierarchy, quiet surfaces, and familiar controls that disappear into the task.

## Anti-references

Avoid colorful or purple-tinted chrome, ornamental gradients, glossy glass effects, oversized controls, excessive card nesting, decorative motion, and visual treatments that compete with conversation content. Do not remove or obscure advanced functionality in pursuit of minimalism.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the conversation and current task visually dominant.
2. Preserve expert control while reducing decorative and chromatic noise.
3. Use one consistent component vocabulary across chat, lists, drawers, forms, and settings.
4. Prefer familiar native affordances and predictable spacing over novelty.
5. Make hierarchy legible through typography, tone, and whitespace before adding containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high text and control contrast, visible disabled and selected states, clear touch targets, native accessibility labels, system font scaling, and existing reduced-motion behavior. Meaning must never depend on color alone.
